Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 1, 2026

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Z Downloader is designed with strict privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, browsing history, downloaded files, or network activity. Everything happens locally on your device.

2. Licensing and Authentication

To verify Z Downloader PRO licenses, the extension communicates securely with our payment provider (Lemon Squeezy). Only your License Key and an anonymous Instance ID are sent to verify the 3-device limit.

3. Web Store Compliance

This extension adheres to Google Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies. Downloading videos from YouTube is strictly prohibited and blocked at the code level.
