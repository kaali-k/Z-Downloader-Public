# Z Downloader - Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** September 1, 2026

## 1. Information We Do Not Collect
Z Downloader is designed with strict privacy in mind. We do **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, browsing history, downloaded files, or network activity. Everything happens locally on your device.

## 2. Licensing and Authentication
To verify Z Downloader PRO licenses, the extension communicates securely with our payment provider (Lemon Squeezy).
- Only your License Key and an anonymous generated Instance ID are sent to Lemon Squeezy to verify the 3-device limit.
- We do not have access to your credit card information.

## 3. Web Store Compliance
This extension strictly adheres to the Google Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies.
- **YouTube Downloading:** Downloading videos from YouTube is strictly prohibited and blocked at the code level to comply with YouTube's Terms of Service and Chrome Web Store policies.

## 4. Contact Us
If you have any questions about your privacy, please contact us at: zentixteamofficial@gmail.com
